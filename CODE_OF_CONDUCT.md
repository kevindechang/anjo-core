# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project respectful and harassment-free,
regardless of background, identity, experience, education, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be precise and kind in technical disagreement.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy; never post real conversation data or credentials.
- Disclose generated or synthetic datasets clearly.
- Accept maintainer decisions on project scope without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
privacy violations, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers
may edit or remove content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports will be
handled as privately as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
